Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave is credited at the start of each quarter, six days at a time. Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward into the next year; anything above that lapses on 31 December.

Sick leave is separate from annual leave. Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any sick leave longer than two consecutive working days.

Interns are entitled to one day of paid leave for every month of their internship. Intern leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

Parental leave: birthing parents receive 26 weeks of paid leave and non-birthing parents receive 4 weeks. Parental leave can begin up to two weeks before the expected due date.

All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least five working days in advance, except for sick leave, which should be reported to the reporting manager on the same day.
