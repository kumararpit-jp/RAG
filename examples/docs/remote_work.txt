Remote Work Guidelines

Employees who have completed their probation period may work from home up to two days per week, subject to approval from their reporting manager. Teams agree on common office days so that in-person collaboration is not disrupted.

During the first three months, new joiners and interns are expected to work from the office every day so that they can be onboarded properly. Exceptions require written approval from both the manager and HR.

When working remotely, employees must be reachable on the company chat between 10 AM and 5 PM, which are the core collaboration hours. Company data may only be accessed through the corporate VPN, and public Wi-Fi networks should be avoided.

The company reimburses up to 1,500 rupees per month towards home internet costs for employees approved for regular remote work. Claims are submitted with the monthly expense report.
